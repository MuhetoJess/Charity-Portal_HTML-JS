About Gabira Giving Portal

Gabira was created to make giving simple, transparent, and personal. We connect individual donors directly with vetted local charities, so every pledge can be tracked from the moment it's made to the moment it reaches the people it's meant to help.

Our Mission

To remove the friction from charitable giving by giving both donors and charities a shared, easy-to-use dashboard — so generosity is matched with accountability.

Our Vision

A community where every donor can see the impact of their giving, and every registered charity has the tools to manage donors, pledges, and campaigns without needing a technical team.
